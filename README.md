## 🔥 Key Features

- **🎮 Smooth Canvas Gameplay:** Responsive HTML5 2D Canvas engine supporting high-DPI displays and smooth 60fps rendering.
- **🛠 Zero External Dependencies:** Built 100% with native browser APIs (`HTML Canvas`, `Web Audio API`, `LocalStorage`).
- **🎵 Synthesized Sound & VFX:** Procedural audio synthesis for sound effects without loading heavy audio files, coupled with custom particle fireworks and confetti systems.
- **🕹 Multiple Control Schemes:** Supports Keyboard (`WASD` / Arrow Keys), Mouse tracking, and Mobile Touch Drag controls.
- **🏆 Progression & Rank System:** Gain XP, level up your rank (Rookie to Legend), complete daily missions, and earn score-based Medals (Bronze to Diamond).
- **🛍 Cosmetics Shop:** Earn in-game coins to unlock custom spaceships and colorful particle engine trails.
- **🎰 Lucky Spin:** Free post-run spin wheel to win coins, XP, and rare cosmetics.
- **📊 Local Leaderboard & Reviews:** Local persistent top 10 score tracker and star rating system.

## 🕹 How to Play

1. **Move:** Use **WASD** / **Arrow Keys**, **Mouse**, or **Touch Drag** (on Mobile) to steer your ship.
2. **Dodge:** Avoid Pink Darts, Orange Tracking Hunters, and Violet Zigzag Enemies.
3. **Collect Orbs:** Grab Gold Orbs to gain points and build your Combo Multiplier.
4. **Power-ups:**
   - 🛡️ **Shield:** Protects you from 1 hit.
   - ⏳ **Slow-mo:** Delays enemy movements.
   - 💣 **Bomb:** Clears all enemies from the screen instantly.
5. **Pause/Mute:** Press `P` or `Esc` to pause, and `M` to toggle sound.
